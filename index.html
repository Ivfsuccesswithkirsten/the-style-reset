Questions

Is this a colour analysis?

It gives you a starting palette based on your answers about your skin, hair and contrast. It's a great place to start and test in daylight, but it isn't the same as an in-person colour analysis.

Do I need to upload photos of myself?

No. Everything works from your answers and a quick tap-list of what's in your wardrobe.

Will it tell me to buy loads of new clothes?

The opposite. Your outfits are built from what you own. The shopping list shows only the few pieces that unlock the most outfits, and if your budget is zero it'll tell you how to borrow or swap.
